## Brand & Style

The platform embodies a unique synthesis: government-partnered institutional gravity paired with high-velocity Silicon Valley product precision. Designed for higher education ecosystems—spanning students, institutes, industry recruiters, and academic faculty—the interface communicates unassailable trust, data sovereignty, and modern algorithmic intelligence. 

The aesthetic is Modern Corporate with sleek, elevated minimalism. It intentionally shuns generic stock photography or frivolous vector illustrations in favor of structured data widgets, live contextual previews, and crisp information architecture. Clean, clinical white backdrops paired with fine hairline dividers (`#E2E8F0`) and ambient, wide-spread diffuse elevation ground the experience. Personality is delivered dynamically via role-based accents, allowing each user pathway to feel purpose-built while retaining a unified platform framework.

## Colors

The foundation is built on absolute clarity: a pure `#FFFFFF` background complemented by high-contrast Slate `#0F172A` headings and `#475569` body copy. Hairline dividers use Slate 200 (`#E2E8F0`) to ensure clean spatial separation without visual weight.

The system utilizes role-based chromatic theming:
- **Student Path (Primary Base):** Royal Indigo (`#4F46E5` to `#6366F1`) signifies intellectual ambition, trust, and future-forward mobility.
- **Institute Path:** Amber-Orange (`#F59E0B` to `#EA580C`) denotes academic governance, operational oversight, and institutional legacy.
- **Industry Hub:** Emerald Green (`#10B981` to `#059669`) highlights enterprise recruitment, verification, and economic capital.
- **Faculty Cell:** Deep Purple (`#8B5CF6` to `#7C3AED`) reflects academic tenure, research excellence, and domain mentorship.

Global validation and institutional trust markers are reserved strictly for Emerald Mint (`#10B981`), backed with `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.10)` on badges, verified transcript seals, and high-confidence AI match percentages.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for commanding, geometric headings with **Inter** for dense, enterprise data legibility.

- **Hero & Primary Headings:** Use `hero-h1` with tight negative tracking (`-0.03em`). The terminal 2–3 words of the primary hero lockup are invariably wrapped in an accent span colored with the active role’s gradient or tone.
- **Eyebrow / Overlines:** Rendered in `eyebrow` uppercase with wide tracking (`0.08em`) to demarcate government initiatives, program badges, and organizational levels.
- **Body Elements:** Constrained strictly to `max-w-[65ch]` in Slate 600 (`#475569`) to guarantee effortless horizontal scanning across analytical views.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by a fluid 12-column grid constrained to an enterprise container ceiling of `80rem` (1280px / `max-w-7xl`).

- **Vertical Cadence:** Major dashboard modules and landing view sections apply a strict `5rem` (`section-padding-y`) separation, establishing an expansive, authoritative cadence.
- **Hero Grid System:** Divided into an asymmetric or 50/50 2-column format (`grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-8`). The left column houses the overline, headline, value proposition, and primary action group. The right column is strictly reserved for live interactive widget cards, verified match readouts, or telemetry nodes—never decorative stock photography.
- **Responsive Behavior:** 
  - Desktop (>1024px): 12-column layout with 24px/32px gutters and 64px outer margin.
  - Tablet (768px–1023px): 8-column layout with 16px gutters and 32px margins; multi-card grids reflow to 2 columns.
  - Mobile (<767px): Single-column stacking with 16px lateral padding. Metric cards scroll horizontally or collapse into full-width cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined "flat-elevated" aesthetic. Heavy drop shadows and dark vignettes are strictly avoided. Depth is achieved via crisp borders, subtle background tints, and soft ambient drop shadows:

- **Interactive Card Elevation:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px solid hairline border in `#E2E8F0`, underpinned by a soft, wide spread shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`.
- **Raised Interactive State:** Hovering interactive cards gently elevates them with `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` and subtle border transition.
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts:** Surface sits over a tinted background scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.40)`) with `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`.
- **Top Utility Banner:** Anchored cleanly at the top boundary (`h-8`) with a flat `#F8FAFC` background, subtle bottom divider `#E2E8F0`, and crisp `#475569` text, providing institutional framing.

## Shapes

A balanced `roundedness: 2` (base radius of 0.5rem / 8px) anchors the platform with corporate rigor:

- **Cards & Data Panels:** Formed with `rounded-xl` (12px to 16px) to maintain soft precision without feeling playful.
- **Form Controls & Buttons:** Use `rounded-lg` (8px) for compact, structural definition.
- **Status Pills, Match Score Counters, and Micro-Indicators:** Retain full pill rounding (`rounded-full`) to contrast sharply against rectilinear data tables and dashboard structures.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Styled in the user’s designated Role Accent color (e.g., `#4F46E5` for Student, `#10B981` for Industry). Rendered with high-contrast white text, `rounded-lg` (or `rounded-xl` in primary hero sections), `px-6 py-3`, and `font-semibold`. Hover introduces a subtle -1px translateY and a calibrated shadow.
- **Secondary CTA:** Transparent background, `border border-[#E2E8F0]`, `#0F172A` text, hovering into a soft `#F8FAFC` background.
- **Utility / Action Icons:** Subtle square/pill buttons with Slate 400 icons that illuminate to the active role accent on hover.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Government / AI Verified Seal:** Always utilizes the global status pair: `color: #10B981` with `background: rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.10)`. Enclosed in a full pill shape (`rounded-full`), framed with an optional 6px solid pulsing green center dot indicator.
- **Category & Role Pills:** Muted slate backgrounds (`#F1F5F9`) with `#475569` body text, shifting to dynamic tint when activated.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 44px, `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, text in `#0F172A`, placeholder in Slate 400 (`#94A3B8`). Focus ring displays a 2px outer aura colored with the active role accent (`rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.20)` for Students).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px x 18px, 1.5px border `#CBD5E1`. Checked state transitions directly to the active role fill with a crisp white glyph.

### Cards & Interactive Widgets
- **Platform Mockup Card:** Designed as the hero visual and primary dashboard unit. Pure white background, `rounded-[16px]`, hairline `#E2E8F0` border, and `0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)` shadow. Header bars display tabular tabs, user verification badges, and dynamic SVG circular progress indicators (e.g., "94% AI Match Score").
- **Kanban & Pipeline Units:** Clean card containers featuring skill tags, applicant identity, GPA verification status, and a 1-click CTA button aligned to the bottom right.
